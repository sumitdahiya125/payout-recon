Chronon finance tooling

Payout reconciliation

Upload the weekly payout register and the bank statement. Every payout is matched or explained, and the workbook ties back to the totals of both files.

What the report contains

  • Summary & Tie-Out: High-level dashboard showing tie-out status (`PASSED`/`FAILED`), category totals, and auto-detected column mappings.
  • Payout Reconciliation: Unified ledger of all payouts with category styling and Excel AutoFilter.
  • Bank Statement Activity: Audit ledger of non-payout and review bank lines with AutoFilter.
  • Data Quality Log: Complete audit trail of skipped, coerced, or suspicious cells.
